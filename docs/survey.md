Pre-purchase condition-and-value survey conducted while the vessel was named
**TIVOLI**. Everything below is drawn from the surveyor's report; it records the
boat as she was on **March 14, 2025**, not necessarily as she is today. Work
completed since then belongs in the [Vessel Changelog](changelog.md).

> Subject to correction of the A and B findings, the surveyor considered the
> vessel reasonably suitable for its intended use.

**Conditions and limitations.** It rained throughout the survey, which prevented
any moisture-meter testing and hindered visual inspection of some exterior
surfaces. Hull inspection was also partly obstructed by the travel-lift straps
and by the antifouling coating. No mechanical/engine survey and no rig survey
were performed — the surveyor recommends a full rigging inspection by a
qualified rigger before the vessel goes offshore.

## 2. A — First Priority / Safety and Compliance

Deficiencies that should be addressed **before the vessel is next underway**.

- [ ] **A-1 — USCG documentation expired.** The Certificate of Documentation
      aboard was expired and no current certificate was aboard.
      *Renew the documentation and keep the current certificate aboard, or
      apply for state registration.* (46 CFR 67)
- [ ] **A-2 — Fire extinguishers untagged.** Three Type ABC-I 2.5 lb dry chemical
      extinguishers aboard, none with a current annual inspection tag.
      *Have them inspected and recertified annually, or replace.*
      (33 CFR 175.310; NFPA 10 Ch. 4)
- [ ] **A-3 — No sound signalling device.** None sighted; required in U.S. waters.
      *Provide a USCG-approved sound signalling device.* (33 CFR 83)
- [ ] **A-4 — Port and starboard navigation lights missing.** The side running
      lights were removed and uninstalled.
      *Install dedicated side navigation lights.* (33 CFR 83)
- [ ] **A-5 — No CO detector in the forward accommodation.** One CO detector was
      installed in the aft accommodation and sounded on test; the forward space
      had none. *Install a CO detector in the forward accommodation.* (ABYC A-24.7)
- [ ] **A-6 — No smoke detectors.** None installed in any accommodation space.
      *Install UL 217-listed single-station smoke alarms in the accommodation
      spaces.* (NFPA 302 §12.3)

## 3. B — Secondary Priority / Timely Attention

Should be corrected in the near future for compliance, condition and value.

### Compliance paperwork

- [ ] **B-1 — Trash disposal placard missing.** Required on vessels over 26'.
      Fine for non-compliance. (33 CFR 151.59)
- [ ] **B-2 — Written waste management plan missing.** Required on vessels over
      39' 4". (MARPOL Annex V; 33 CFR 151.57)
- [ ] **B-3 — Navigation Rules handbook missing.** The USCG International and
      Inland Navigation Rules handbook (Nav-Rules CG169, the 72 COLREGS) is
      required aboard vessels 12 m / 39' 4" and larger. (33 CFR 83)

### Hull, deck and rig

- [ ] **B-5 — Keel separated at the trailing edge.** The aft trailing edge of the
      keel has separated from the hull. *Set the vessel on the hard with the keel
      just touching the ground, adjust the keel bolts with a torque wrench, and
      re-caulk the keel/hull seam with bedding compound.*
- [ ] **B-6 — Teak deck caulking lifting.** The caulking sealant in the teak
      overlays (cockpit seats and swim platform) was slightly degraded and
      lifting in places.
- [ ] **B-7 — Stanchion bases loose.** Some perimeter railing stanchions were
      loose at their bases, **creating leaks below**. *Investigate, re-bed and
      refit; confirm they are properly sealed.*
- [ ] **B-8 — Starboard aft handrail adrift.** *Secure the handrail to the stanchion.*
- [ ] **B-9 — Transom lifeline missing.** The single stainless lifeline at the
      transom is missing. *Replace.*
- [ ] **B-17 — Zinc anodes wasted.** The underwater anodes were wasting or wasted.
      *Replace to restore galvanic protection.*

### Machinery and systems

- [ ] **B-4 — LPG tank unsecured.** The propane tank in the aft port cockpit
      transom locker was not secured. *Install a secure mount.*
- [ ] **B-10 — No heat shield on the Racor bowl.** The Racor 500-FG primary fuel
      filter had no approved heat shield under its sight glass bowl.
      *Install a metallic shield meeting ASTM F1201 with the appropriate flame rating.*
- [ ] **B-11 — Battery terminals uncovered.** The engine start battery's terminals
      had no protective insulating covers. *Install per 33 CFR 183.420 to prevent
      accidental shorting.*
- [ ] **B-12 — Wind generator control panel not found.** The control panel for the
      Air-X Marine wind generator was not sighted. *Investigate; reinstate,
      service or replace.*
- [ ] **B-13 — Galley saltwater foot pump disconnected.** The Whale mechanical
      foot pump is disconnected and non-operational.
- [ ] **B-14 — Freshwater strainer fouled.** The inline strainer at the freshwater
      pump was fouled/dirty. *Clean.*

### Electronics

- [ ] **B-15 — Sonar function faulty.** The sonar function of the Furuno NavNet
      MFD did not fully function. *Investigate and trace; service, repair or replace.*
- [ ] **B-16 — Depth/speed displays dead.** Neither Robertson Dateline depth/speed
      display powered up when tested.

## 4. C — General Findings and Observations

Lower priority or cosmetic; address in keeping with good maintenance practice.

- [ ] **C-1 — Bulkhead wood damage.** Wood damage was sighted on the athwartship
      bulkheads in the forward accommodation. *Repair in accordance with good
      marine practice.*

Also noted in the body of the report but not assigned a finding number:

- Interior mirrors show general desilvering of the reflective coating.
- Mild surface corrosion on the engine pulley mechanisms and on the motor mounts.
- The propeller shaft coupler was **not safety-wired**; the surveyor recommends a
  corrosion inhibitor on the coupler bolts.
- The strut's cutless bearing showed slight wear — monitor and replace as necessary.
- No bonding system is installed (ABYC E-2 / E-11).
- The life raft is reported to be part of the ship's inventory but is **currently
  out of service** and was not sighted at the survey.
- The EPIRB was not tested; it needs re-registration under new ownership.
