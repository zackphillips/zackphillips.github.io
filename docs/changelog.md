## 2025

### 2025-03-14 | Other | Pre-purchase marine survey
- Condition-and-value survey by Capt. Rick Whiting (SAMS AMS), Starboard Marine — file 9382 Rev. 1. Hauled at KKMI Boatyard, Richmond, CA.
- Overall rating **average condition**; fair market value $79,000.
- 6 first-priority (A) findings, 17 secondary (B) findings, 1 general (C) finding.
- Engine hours at survey: **6,376**.
- Full report: [2025 Marine Survey](survey.md). Log repairs as they are completed.

### 2025-05-XX | Electronics | Vessel tracker deployed
- Launched [mermug.com](https://mermug.com) — Raspberry Pi aboard running SignalK → GitHub Pages pipeline.
- SignalK server running on `192.168.8.50:3000`.
- Data update cadence: ~150 seconds.
- Privacy zone configured for South Beach Harbor (200 m radius).
