# S.V. Mermug — Captain's Log

A running, chronological log of voyages — who was aboard, what the
conditions were like, and anything worth remembering — plus any maintenance
done underway. For the full technical history of repairs, upgrades and
installs, see the [Vessel Changelog](changelog.md); for what's coming due,
see [Scheduled Maintenance](maintenance.md).
